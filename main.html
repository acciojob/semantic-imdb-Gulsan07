Inception

Actors | Quotes
Inception is a science fiction thriller movie directed by Christopher Nolan. The story follows Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who enters people's dreams to steal secrets. He is given a chance to erase his criminal history by planting an idea into someone's mind.

Actors

Leonardo DiCaprio plays Dom Cobb, the main character of the movie. Other famous actors include Joseph Gordon-Levitt, Tom Hardy, and Ellen Page.
